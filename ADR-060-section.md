## ADR-060 · Destination health and degraded reclaim: the stage's role B drops all-or-nothing in favour of per-destination health-gated proof with an at-least-one guard, and the stage cap warns but never acts (2026-10-22 drafted; direction + defaults Dim-approved 2026-10-20, 46 §0 / 47 §7; 正手 approved with mandatory edits 2026-10-22 — A1, A2 and all seven open-point rulings folded in)

**Status**: ✅ **已批准（带修改）** — 正手 2026-10-22（`ADR-060-061-REVIEW.md`, INBOX〔ADR-REVIEW〕）：必改 A1（每目的地可覆盖降级阈值）与建议 A2（降级报警永远给两条修法）已并入，7 条开放点全部裁定（正文各处标 [正手 2026-10-22 裁定]）。方向与默认值 Dim 2026-10-20 批（46 §0「都同意」：B3 降级回收改 ADR-020 判据 · 7 天 / 10 GB 默认；47 §7「都批准」：撤回 46 §8.4 改为 §5.3）。实现（SG-2）可即刻开工；**上线闸 = LM-1**（D6）。评审依据的底稿：`ADR-060-destination-health-degraded-reclaim-DRAFT.md`（adr-drafts, 2026-10-22, W1013）。本节由 W1018 落稿，未做裁决外的增删。

### Context

1. **One bad destination deadlocks the whole machine's reclaim — measured.** [fact] The weekly reclaim on m1max has been BLOCKED since 2026-10-04 with `destination r2 unreachable — 9869 sessions / 12.1 GiB held back`; the stage held 21 GB growing ~0.29 GB/hour, ~7 GB/week (opencode alone 6.5 of 7.2 GB), disk at 93 GB free [46 §2]. The code reason, quoted: `stagereclaim.rs:8-15` — "The hard rule, first and only: **no shard is deleted until every declared destination proves it holds that session**", and "a destination that cannot be consulted is *unproven*, which blocks the reclaim: it is never treated as 'does not have it' (that would delete data) nor as 'has it' (that would guess)". `cmd_reclaim_stage` proves against *every* declared destination by construction (`main.rs:11440-11482`; `schedule` refuses `--destination` with unit `reclaim-stage`, `main.rs:8500-8501`), and a blocked run deletes nothing and exits 1.
2. **Why this is the *good* bad case: every config mistake looked reasonable.** [fact, 46 §1] Two destinations declared, one hourly timer carrying only the healthy one; creds for the second in shell env vars that launchd never inherits; one reclaim timer governing all destinations; the only signal a log nobody reads while `status` stayed green (SG-1 has since made the three surfaces speak — C1). The product promise this ADR serves (46 §1): a config error must not stop the working destination from taking backups, must not remove the stage's bound, and must be loud about the broken one.
3. **What all-or-nothing was protecting, and what actually protects it.** [46 §5.2 debate, Dim-approved] During R2's outage the fleet de facto had one off-site copy anyway — the stage is local, never an off-site copy; "两份异地" is protected by the *alert* layer (SG-1, landed), not by stage retention. Recovery for a destination that comes back is ADR-013's union backfill (local re-collect first, then "the other destinations have, local no longer has" — enabled over reclaimed bodies by ADR-021's cumulative read), so the stage is redundant as the recovery source. [inference] The remaining risk B3 accepts: while a degraded destination stays down, its copy of the *still-increment* era lives only on the healthy destinations — bounded by the at-least-one guard plus the alerts.
4. **What stays byte-for-byte.** [fact] ADR-020 Phase 4 / ADR-012: proof asks the archive for the per-session digest triple `(shard_count, concat_bytes, concat_sha256)`, never a cursor; the retained summaries persist **before** bodies delete; deletion is per-session, resumable, idempotent; dry-run is the default and `--apply` is the operator's explicit act; the shard-seq high-water mark is never reused (ADR-020 criterion 2; the pre-reclaim crash window re-proves everything on the next run).
5. **SG-1 already built the observation layer this ADR consumes.** [fact] `destdiag.rs` computes four facts per declared destination (`PushCoverage` timers, `CredentialResolution` in the *job's* env, `LastPush` from `push-state.json` (`At { finished_at_unix, snapshots_in_repo }` / `NoRecord` / `Unknown`), `ReclaimShare` from the latest reclaim record), shared-wording alerts on `status` / `doctor` / menubar; `pushstate.rs` keeps the per-destination durable record written **only by `cmd_push` on success** (no record worded as absence, never "never pushed"; unknown version ⇒ unreadable, never healthy; a corrupt record already alerts with its fix — W1012g, and the next successful push rewrites it); `reclaimstate.rs` keeps the weekly `reclaim-state.json`; the ADR-023 classifier's verdict rides inside `BlockedKind::Unreachable`.
6. **The local-full-copy interplay already moved.** [fact] ADR-020 Phase 5 (= ADR-001 ⑥ switch) plus 46 §8.4's initial "switch on ⇒ reclaim off" was **withdrawn** by 47 §5.3 the same day, Dim approving (47 §7: 「撤回 46 §8.4 改为 §5.3」): the local full copy becomes the mirror's job (ADR-062 / LM), reclaim proceeds, and with a mirror on, a session may only be reclaimed after the mirror has synced to a snapshot containing it. 47 §6 sets the ordering: **SG-2 goes live only after LM-1** —「SG-2（回收）要等 LM-1 的条件改完再上线，避免两个 ADR 打架」.

### Decision

**D1 — role B, amended (ADR-020's five-role table row).** Old: "B · debt voucher (ADR-013) — kept until **every declared destination** is proven to hold." New: kept until **every healthy declared destination is proven to hold it, and at least one declared destination is proven to hold it**; degraded destinations neither participate in the proof nor extend the retention, and are listed with their health and their stats on **every** run (46 §5.2 B3's exact conditions). ADR-020 acceptance criterion 1 re-reads: *no shard is deleted before the archive is proven to hold it — enforced for every healthy declared destination, with at least one proof overall*; criterion 5 (a blocked reclaim must speak) is unchanged and now real (SG-1). The two clauses are kept as separate formal guards because they answer different questions — who **must** prove (protection of every working destination) vs. **that a proof exists** (the sole-destination case: only one declared and it is broken ⇒ delete nothing, the ≥1 clause holds the bottom, 46 §5.2). [Dim-approved direction, 46 §0/§8.1]

**D2 — destination health: derived, never stored; three reportable states plus a fail-safe fourth — each judged against its own effective degrade-after.**
- `healthy` — last successful push within that destination's **effective degrade-after**: `[destinations.<name>] degrade_after_days` when the destination declares one, else the global `[reclaim] degrade_after_days` (default **7 days** = the weekly reclaim cadence; configurable; 46 §5.2/§8.2). **Per-destination override is part of the approved decision [正手 2026-10-22, 必改 A1]:** `destinations.md` itself recommends an external disk as a second destination, and such a destination is naturally pushed by hand about once a month. Under only a global 7-day window it would sit `lagging` forever and alarm on every run, and the user learns to ignore alarms — the exact failure C1 exists to prevent. Declared its own rhythm, it is healthy on that rhythm and **still participates in the proof**; past its own threshold it degrades and alarms exactly like the global rule. This is not the rejected per-destination exclusion switch (Rejected, last bullet): no destination can silently lose protection — only "how behind is behind" follows the user's declared rhythm.
- `lagging` — last successful push older than that: assumed to be missing the recent increment era; not consulted (D3), alarming every run — and the alarm's fix advice **always names both roads**: repair the push (with the exact commands), or remove the destination from the config if it is no longer needed. Never one road only. [正手 2026-10-22, 建议 A2]
- `never-pushed` — no record. Prose rule inherited verbatim from `pushstate.rs`/`destdiag.rs`: worded as **"no push on record"**（「没有推送记录」）, never "never pushed" (a machine that pushed before this CLI kept records leaves the same absence). State slug in `--json` **is** `never-pushed`; prose must not lie by suggestiveness. [正手 2026-10-22 裁定（草案开放点 4）]
- `unknown` — record present but unreadable/unknown version: **must-prove**, decided (fail-safe: an unreadable record may never become deletion permission — the unknown-never-empty invariant). The W1012g alert already points at the fix (next successful push rewrites it), and it must co-appear with the reclaim's own refusal. [正手 2026-10-22 裁定（草案开放点 1）]
  - Classification is one function, homed beside the C1 facts in `destdiag.rs`, parameterized by the `now` value (never a clock read inside the computation — the destdiag testability rule [fact]); both `reclaim-stage` and the status surfaces call the same one, so the three surfaces and the reclaim can never disagree about health (the shared-wording half of C1 applied to health). Health heals automatically with the next successful push — a degraded state is computed, never latched. Degradation never affects push targeting: a lagging destination keeps receiving pushes exactly as before (ADR-022's meta-hash change detection and the empty-stage gate untouched).
  - A destination filled by `dest-init` but not yet pushed reads no record ⇒ `never-pushed`: intended — it has not received this machine's increments; the first push within its declared rhythm turns it healthy. [inference; accepted by the 46 §4.6 backfill acceptance shape]

**D3 — proof participation: consult the healthy set only.** The proof walk visits every **healthy** declared destination with today's per-destination consult + triple-match semantics (`BlockedKind::Unreachable / PartialRead / SessionNotHeld / TripleMismatch` each still block, for the healthy set only). Degraded (`lagging` / `never-pushed`) destinations are **not consulted** — 46 §5.2 B3's own words:「lagging / never-pushed 的不参与证明，但每一次都被列出来报警」:
- not consulting dead destinations removes the weekly connect-failure surface (the raw third-party "We believe this is a bug" chain no longer aborts the run; its classified verdict still feeds the C1③ facts);
- a degraded-but-reachable destination's absences prove nothing — it is missing the recent era *by construction*, so a consult can only confirm expected absences while costing a cumulative read (the 22-GB-scan minutes this ADR removes);
- degradation is loud instead of blocking: health, the four facts, and the fix path appear in the report on every run and in the three surfaces (D7 keeps them distinct; the fix path is the two-road advice of D2).
With degraded destinations unconsulted, the ≥1 clause is equivalent to "the healthy set is non-empty"; the clauses are still written separately (D1). [Dim-approved, 46 §5.2]

**D4 — recovery backfill reuses dest-init; the stage is never the recovery source.** A destination that becomes healthy again gets what it is missing exactly the way a new destination does: ADR-013's union (local re-collect first, then copy "held elsewhere, absent locally" from the healthy destinations), which ADR-021's cumulative read makes independent of the stage retaining old bodies. Cross-destination masterkeys differ by design (`masterkey-r2` / `masterkey-storagebox` here) — that is a dest-init property, not a new mechanism. [fact, 46 §5.2 risk note] Large-repo backfill duration is a measured-before-close item, not a design unknown: SG-5's acceptance (46 §4.6) is the measurement (R2 catches up to local ∪ storagebox, both `verify` L3 green, no dependence on old bodies in the stage).

**D5 — the stage cap: alarm only, never an agent of anything; config: `[reclaim]` globals plus one per-destination override key.** New config section `[reclaim]` [正手 2026-10-22 裁定（草案开放点 3）] with `degrade_after_days` (default 7) and `stage_cap_bytes` (default `10GiB`; parsed with the cache.max_bytes dual spelling — `"10GB"` = 10⁹, `"10GiB"` = 2³⁰, so a value meant as one thing cannot be read as the other). `degrade_after_days` is additionally overridable per destination: `[destinations.<name>] degrade_after_days` overrides the global value for that destination (D2, 必改 A1). Over the cap ⇒ **red** severity in `status` / `doctor` / menubar through the SG-1 alert channel: current bytes, the cap, the advice line with the fix commands. Nothing else happens:
- the original "still over ⇒ stop collecting" arm is rejected (stopping collection stops backups, which is worse than a full disk — 46 §5.2's own ruling);
- the cap adds no new reclaim trigger either — the standing weekly job already runs B3; an over-cap stage tells the user their *proofs* are stuck, which is the healthy-set/cap alarms' job to say, not a byte threshold's;
- size basis, decided [正手 2026-10-22 裁定（草案开放点 5）]: shard-body bytes only (the sessions tree), stat-only walk, `meta/` excluded (measured ~10 MB — noise [46 §2]); reported even while reclaim is blocked, so held-back backlog and cap alarms are two visible, separately-worded facts.
[46 §8.2 set the 10-GB default question; 46 §5.2 B4's residue is exactly the alarm-only form Dim approved]

**D6 — mirror and Phase 5: reclaim proceeds; the condition cites 47 §5.3.** A session may leave the stage when (i) D1/D3's proof holds, **and** (ii) if a mirror of a destination is configured and on, the mirror has already synced to a snapshot containing that session (47 §5.3's own words: no "stage deleted, mirror hasn't pulled it yet" window — any moment the machine can read the session offline). The mirror never counts as a destination and never as proof (47 §5.3: 本地镜像不算异地目的地，不参与远端证明). 46 §8.4 ("switch open ⇒ reclaim disabled") **stands withdrawn, decided** [正手 2026-10-22 裁定（草案开放点 2）] — the ADR encodes 47 §5.3 and does not carry the superseded rule; recorded so no implementation resurrects it, and 46 keeps its 🔁 note. The mirror-side machinery (sync, watermark, status) is ADR-062/LM-2's to define; this ADR owns only the precondition hook at the reclaim gate, config-gated so it is a no-op until a mirror exists (exercised for real at LM-5's offline acceptance). **Shipping gate (依赖注明): SG-2's implementation goes live only after LM-1 (ADR-062 draft + its §5.3 condition revision) has been reviewed — 47 §6's exact ordering rule. Implementation may proceed on the branch while LM-1 is in review; if LM-1's reviewed text differs from §5.3's wording, D6 is re-checked before ship.** [Dim-approved, 47 §7] The review's own ordering note stands: ADR-062's draft review is scheduled ahead of SG-2's ship window so SG-2 does not idle (见 Tickets).

**D7 — report and durable records keep unknown-never-empty and the exit-code contract.** (CLAUDE.md invariants 1–2.)
- `ReclaimReport` gains, per declared destination: health state, proof role (`must-prove` / `degraded-not-consulted`), and — for the must-prove set — today's blocked kinds unchanged. The new shape names are as drafted [正手 2026-10-22 裁定（草案开放点 6）]: `destdiag`'s `ReclaimShare` gains the visible **`degraded-not-consulted`** arm, the empty-proof-set outcome keeps the wording below with the refusal exit, and the `--json` health vocabulary is D2's four states (`healthy` / `lagging` / `never-pushed` / `unknown`).
- Folding degradation into `NotBlocking` (which today means "proved or not among the blocked") would make a state the user must see disappear — the exact shape this invariant exists to forbid; hence the separate arm.
- An empty healthy set (no possible prover) is its own blocked shape + prose — "no healthy destination to prove against; nothing deleted; restoration path: fix pushes, health heals automatically" — never 0-with-success, never silence; exit code stays the refusal code for it.
- Degraded destinations appear with health, the four facts, and the two-road fix advice (D2) in the report and all three surfaces.
- `reclaim-state.json` gains the run's per-destination health snapshot (what the run actually saw), shape-versioned so an unknown version reads unreadable-⇒-alert, never healthy — **the W1012g policy applied forward, confirmed** [正手 2026-10-22 裁定（草案开放点 7）].

**D8 — tests this ADR requires (46 §4.7's first half; the boundary drawn):**
- **The §4.7 fixture**: one never-pushed destination + one healthy destination declared; the healthy one holds all candidates ⇒ reclaim proceeds (dry-run and apply), the never-pushed one is reported degraded **and** alarmed with the shared wording — the two-road fix advice included (the three surfaces say what the report says), readback from the healthy destination is byte-identical, and L3 passes on the retained summaries — no data loss.
- **The A1 fixture**: a destination declaring `[destinations.<name>] degrade_after_days = 35` whose last successful push is 20 days old ⇒ classified `healthy` and inside the **must-prove** set — consulted like any healthy destination, able to block; no degraded alarm fires. [正手 2026-10-22, 必改 A1 的夹具：覆盖为 35 天的目的地 20 天没推 ⇒ healthy、必须证明]
- Variant: the healthy destination missing one candidate ⇒ exactly that session held back (must-prove is per-session; the rest still reclaim; resumability/idempotence unchanged).
- Sole declared destination degraded ⇒ nothing deleted, the loud empty-proof-set outcome (≥1 guard).
- Healthy-but-unreachable ⇒ blocks as today (degradation is time-based, never reachability-based: a young record cannot be outrun by a network blip — 46 §5.2's "太短" debate is why 7 days, not hours).
- Corrupt push-state record ⇒ must-prove (blocks) with the W1012g alert co-present; after a successful push rewrites it, health recovers on the next classification.
- Cap exceeded ⇒ the red cap alarm fires and nothing else changes (run outcome unaffected by the alarm).
- **Boundary note**: the *other* half of 46 §4.7 (a snapshot-source session grown 20× ⇒ stage ≈ final × 1.x; readback byte-identical to the last version) is **ADR-061 / SG-3's** acceptance, recorded here only so the SG-2 review does not lose either half of §4.7.
- Live-machine gate stays 46 §6's: on m1max with the R2 misconfiguration kept intact (review, 顺序与闸: R2 错误配置继续保持), R2 marks degraded, the storagebox-proven sessions reclaim; dry-run first, 正手 reads it, then `--apply`; a 50-session random sample reads back from storagebox byte-identical to the pre-reclaim local bytes (46 §4.1). SG-2's ship waits for LM-1 (D6).

### Consequences

- The W1013-case picture ends: a never-works destination no longer pins 12.1 GiB and a whole machine's boundedness. The stage returns toward "recent unproven increment" (46 §3 target 1), and the weekly job's consult surface shrinks to the destinations that can actually answer.
- A user with one working destination reclaims weekly even with a second broken one; the broken one is loud on three surfaces with one wording; recovery is automatic through the union backfill, no flags to clear.
- A hand-pushed second destination (the external-disk shape `destinations.md` recommends) lives inside its declared rhythm instead of under a permanent alarm: the alarm that remains fires at that rhythm's own edge and past it, which is what keeps alarms meaningful (the C1 premise A1 protects).
- New honest failure mode, by design: a fleet whose every destination is degraded loses reclaim until one becomes healthy — loud, with the fix named. The ≥1 guard is what makes that the *safe* direction.
- Recovery backfill costs a real cumulative read over the network (destinations' key sets differ); measured at SG-5 — the accepted price of deleting bodies a degraded destination never received.
- Config surface grows one section plus one per-destination override key; public docs stay untouched this round — the `destinations.md` reclaim paragraph, `schedule.md`'s reclaim note and the new keys' documentation rewrite under SG-6, where the three-surface consistency gate lives.
- The stage's five roles (ADR-020) rebalance: role E (the accidental local full copy) moves to the mirror for good (ADR-062's territory, per 47 §5.3), role B's retention shrinks to the healthy-set proof, role C survives on the retained summaries, role A is untouched.

### 🔴 Invalidates (必填)

1. **Assumption down:** "every declared destination must prove before any deletion; one unproven destination blocks everything" — the all-or-nothing reading of ADR-020 role B + criterion 1 (`stagereclaim.rs:8-15` module doc; `main.rs:11440-11444` doc). **Dependents named:** the proof walk and `blocked`/`ReclaimReport::blocked()` semantics in `stagereclaim.rs`; `destdiag.rs`'s `ReclaimShare` vocabulary (needs the new arm, D7); `cmd_reclaim_stage`'s blocked ⇒ delete-nothing/exit-1 contract (narrowed to the healthy set, intact for it); the public paragraph `docs/destinations.md:94` — its pair "this fails safe: nothing is deleted. But nothing is reclaimed either" loses both absolutes once the waiting destination passes the threshold (proven sessions *do* get deleted after proof; reclaim *does* proceed), stale in a new way post-SG-1 — rewrite owed by SG-6, not this round.
2. **Assumption down:** "the stage is the recovery source for a destination that was unreachable and comes back" (what all-or-nothing de facto enforced). **Replacement:** the recovery source is the healthy destinations via ADR-013's union + ADR-021's cumulative read; backfill never depends on stage retention. **Dependents:** `destinit.rs` (already cumulative — no change), the SG-5 acceptance (46 §4.6), and any prose advising users to leave the stage alone until all destinations catch up (SG-6 sweep).
3. **Assumption down:** "`push-state.json` is advisory — alerts only." After this ADR it gates deletion (D2's derivation input). **Dependents:** the record's integrity becomes functional (corrupt ⇒ must-prove, blocking, alarmed); `destdiag::LastPush` becomes a reclaim input in addition to an alert source; the write rule stays strict (only `cmd_push` on success heals health — a manual push counts the same way it counts for the C1③ fact).
4. **Withdrawn decision, carried here so it cannot resurrect (Dim approved the withdrawal):** 46 §8.4's "the Phase-5 switch on ⇒ reclaim off." Any implementation written against it re-points at 47 §5.3's mirror-sync precondition (D6). This ADR records the final text; 46 keeps the 🔁 annotation.
5. **Not invalidated — re-affirmed for the review's perimeter:** ADR-012 (archive is the truth; proof asks archives, never cursors); ADR-020 Phase 2 (seq high-water non-reuse), Phase 3 (summaries before bodies), criterion 4 (overview reflects archive), criterion 5 (blocked reclaim speaks); ADR-021 decision 1 (**reclaim's proof keeps the latest-snapshot read** — candidates are still in the stage, so they are still in the newest snapshot; unchanged); ADR-022 (push change detection and the empty-stage gate); ADR-023 (classifier, inside `BlockedKind::Unreachable`); ADR-013 (union order; no default destination — reclaim proves against *all declared*, the health model picks who must answer, never a hand-chosen subset); the append-only archive; exit-code semantics 3/1/2; unknown-never-empty in every state D7 adds.

### Rejected (recorded)

- **B1 — reclaim scoped to what timers push:** withdrawn by 46 §7; couples retention to scheduler installation and misjudges hand-pushers — and would make the A1 case (a legitimately hand-pushed monthly destination) *silently* lose R2-style protection instead of alarming it. (The CLI truth: `reclaim-stage` has no `--destination` scoping and its `schedule` unit refuses one — `main.rs:8500-8501`; none is added.)
- **B2 — all-or-nothing, just louder:** zero data risk, disk still fills; the SG-1 layer now exists and has freed 0 of 12.1 GiB. 46 target 3 unsatisfied. [46 §5.2]
- **B4's stop arm — over cap ⇒ stop collecting:** stopping collection stops backups, worse than a full disk (46 §5.2). Also narrowed away: cap-driven forced reclaim — the weekly job already exists; a byte threshold adds no proof the archive holds anything.
- **Opportunistic consult (degraded destinations consulted, non-blocking, may count toward ≥1):** re-fails dead destinations weekly (noise + connect cost), buys only expected absences from the degraded set, and in the sole-lagging-destination case would greenlight deletion on a fleet that has received nothing in > 7 days — the exact case the ≥1 guard exists to stop. 46's literal 「不参与证明」 is adopted instead.
- **Healthy-by-default fallbacks (NoRecord ⇒ "fresh", unreadable ⇒ lagging, timer presence ⇒ healthy):** each guesses "recently fine" out of an absence or an unrelated fact — the unknown-never-empty invariant and `pushstate.rs`'s own wording rule forbid the first; the second turns corrupt bookkeeping into deletion permission; the third is B1 laundered (a timer proves nothing about *success*; C1's ③ fact exists because of that).
- **A user-settable "exclude this destination from reclaim protection" flag:** 46 §6's manual one-off (a single-destination temporary config) stays a manual act under the incident clause; a shipped flag would let a destination lose protection *silently and forever* with no alarm pressing toward repair. **Not the same thing as A1's `degrade_after_days` override [正手 2026-10-22 必改 A1]:** the override never removes protection from anyone — it only lets "how behind is behind" follow the destination's declared rhythm; over that rhythm the destination degrades and alarms like every other. Revisit trigger below if a real exclusion case surfaces.

### Revisit triggers

1. LM-1's reviewed ADR-062 text diverges from 47 §5.3 in wording or mechanism ⇒ D6 re-checked (pre-ship gate; post-ship, re-opens D6).
2. After SG-3 (ADR-061) lands and stage deltas shrink, re-measure whether the 7-day default still matches the weekly cadence's intent (the value stays config; changing the *default* needs fresh measurement).
3. A real SG-5 backfill materially slower than the growth it serves ⇒ surface its progress (resumable, like dest-init) — presentation, not semantics.
4. Corrupt push-state records seen more than once in the wild ⇒ re-examine the must-prove treatment's DX (it blocks in the safe direction; the one-click fix — a successful push — must remain the documented answer).
5. A genuine user case for protection-exclusion surfaces ⇒ brings its own alarm design (never a silent flag).
